Leadwort.OilPaint.Runtime.Core.OilPaintSettings Struct Reference
Classes

Artist controls for the oil paint filter. The filter runs in four stages: flow detection, abstraction (anisotropic Kuwahara), color quantization and paint relief (brush + varnish).

Public Attributes

float FlowSmoothness

float FlatAreaThreshold
 How much detail a region needs before strokes follow it. Flatter regions (skies, walls) use the Flat Area Stroke Angle instead.
