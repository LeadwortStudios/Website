Leadwort.Diorama.Runtime.Core.DioramaSettings Struct Reference
Classes

Artist controls for the paper diorama: the scene is cut into stacked layers of card that cast soft shadows on each other, with a white cut edge and a paper fiber attached to the surfaces. Sizes in pixels refer to a 1080p output and scale with the resolution.
