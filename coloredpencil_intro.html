Package description

Colored Pencil Filter is a real-time post-processing effect that turns your game into a colored pencil drawing: graphite outlines, hatching that follows the form of every object and builds up in the shadows, and colors laid down with pencil strokes on paper with grain. Unlike most sketch filters, the hatching is attached to the surfaces of your scene: when the camera moves, the strokes move with the world instead of sliding over it.

It is based on research in line drawing, hatching and coherent stylization (Flow-Based Image Abstraction, Kang et al. 2009; XDoG, Winnemöller et al. 2012; Real-Time Hatching, Praun et al. 2001; Dynamic Solid Textures for Real-Time Coherent Stylization, Bénard et al. 2009), adapted to run every frame. It works with both the Built-In Render Pipeline and URP (Universal Render Pipeline), with full support for the Render Graph API of Unity 6 (URP 17).

How it works

Each frame the filter runs five stages, all configured through ColoredPencilSettings. Every stage is a smooth function of the image (no strokes are placed one by one), so nothing pops in or out when the scene moves:

  • Stroke Flow - detects the direction of the shapes in the image (smoothed structure tensor). Hatching runs along this flow; flat areas such as skies use a constant hatching angle.
  • Hatching - noise attached to the 3D surfaces of the scene (reconstructed from the depth buffer, with a constant on-screen stroke width at any distance) is smeared along the flow into pencil strokes, in two layers for cross-hatching. The darker and more colorful an area, the more strokes cover it.
  • Outlines - a difference of Gaussians smoothed along the edges draws graphite contours. Texture Suppression removes lines inside textured surfaces (rock, grass, bark) while keeping the contours of objects.
  • Color & Paper - the strokes use the colors of your scene, saturated like pencil pigments, on a paper with grain.
  • Temporal Stability - blends with the previous frame (reprojected with the depth buffer) to keep the drawing steady in motion, and trusts the history less during fast motion so the image stays sharp.

Where it lives in your project

Depending on your render pipeline, you'll attach a different component:

Pipeline Component
Built-In Render Pipeline ColoredPencilPostProcess (Camera component)
URP (Render Graph) ColoredPencilPostProcessFeature (Renderer Feature)

Both share the same ColoredPencilSettings and ColoredPencilPreset assets, so presets work in either pipeline.
