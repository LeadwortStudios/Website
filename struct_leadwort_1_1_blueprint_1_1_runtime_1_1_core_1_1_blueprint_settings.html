Leadwort.Blueprint.Runtime.Core.BlueprintSettings Struct Reference
Classes

Artist controls for the blueprint filter: technical lines from the geometry and the image, a metric grid on the surfaces, shading, paper, dimension annotations and a title block. Sizes in pixels refer to a 1080p output and scale with the resolution.
