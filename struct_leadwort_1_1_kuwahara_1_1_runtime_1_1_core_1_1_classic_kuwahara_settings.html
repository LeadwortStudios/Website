Classes

Artist controls for the standard painterly effect. Best for a simplified, retro oil-paint style.

Public Attributes

float BrushSize
 Size of the painterly brush strokes. Larger values create bigger, more abstract color blobs.
float BrushSizeSmoothing
 Prevents stuttering or popping visually if you animate the brush size over time.
float EdgeSmoothness
 Blends between a soft, smudged painting (0) and a high-contrast, posterized look (1).
float StrokeSharpness
 Changes the feeling of the brush from round and soft (0) to square and digital (1).
float ColorSensitivity
 Forces the filter to care more about color changes rather than just light/shadow changes.
TextureScale TextureScale
 Drops the internal rendering resolution to heavily boost performance, making the painting look thicker and less precise.
